When your app writes to the local database, the SDK records each mutation in an upload queue. Your _mutation uploader_ uploads the queued mutations to your write API, which applies them to the source database. Add the uploader before your app writes: while the app is connected without an uploader, pending mutations stop synced data from updating, and the sync status reports a download error.

To pick how to upload mutations, see [Choosing Your Implementation](/handling-writes/write-path/choosing-your-implementation).
