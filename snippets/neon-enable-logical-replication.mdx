Enabling [logical replication](https://neon.com/docs/guides/logical-replication-neon) can't be undone. It also restarts your project's computes, which drops active connections.

In the [Neon Console](https://console.neon.tech), go to **Settings** > **Postgres** and enable **Logical Replication**.

<Note>
  While PowerSync is connected, its replication connection keeps your Neon compute active, so the compute doesn't [scale to zero](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/scale-to-zero). On the Neon Free plan, the monthly allowance of 100 CU-hours runs a 0.25 CU compute for about 400 hours.
</Note>
