When your app writes to the local database, the SDK records each mutation in an upload queue. Your _mutation uploader_ uploads the queued mutations to your write API, which applies them to the source database. Add the uploader before your app writes: while the app is connected without an uploader, pending mutations stop synced data from updating, and the sync status reports a download error.

How you implement the uploader depends on your backend. PowerSync supports several options, such as your existing API, Supabase, or a reference write implementation that you can copy. To pick one, see [Choosing Your Implementation](/handling-writes/write-path/choosing-your-implementation): each option gives you a complete `uploadData()`. The example below only shows its general shape: take the next transaction from the upload queue, send its mutations to your write API, and mark it complete.
